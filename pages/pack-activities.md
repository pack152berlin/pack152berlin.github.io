## What Is Cub Scouting?

Cub Scouting is a program for youth in kindergarten through fifth grade. Scouts learn by doing through outdoor adventure, games, crafts, STEM activities, service, ceremonies, and age-appropriate leadership opportunities.

The program helps young people build character, confidence, citizenship, fitness, and practical skills while having fun with friends and family.

## Grade Levels and Dens

Cub Scouts are grouped by school grade into dens. Dens meet as smaller groups, while the whole pack gathers for larger activities and events.

- **Lions**: Kindergarten
- **Tigers**: 1st grade
- **Wolves**: 2nd grade
- **Bears**: 3rd grade
- **Webelos**: 4th grade
- **Arrow of Light**: 5th grade

Each rank includes adventures and requirements that help Scouts grow step by step.

## Learning by Doing

Cub Scouting is hands-on. Scouts might build, hike, cook, serve, perform, race, explore, or solve a challenge together. Activities are designed to be active, age-appropriate, and fun.

## Outdoor Adventure

Outdoor experiences are a central part of Scouting. Pack 152 activities may include hikes, campouts, nature exploration, outdoor games, and Berlin-area adventures. Families learn together, and beginners are welcome.

## Service and Leadership

Service helps Scouts learn that they can make a difference. Leadership begins with small responsibilities: helping a younger Scout, leading a flag ceremony, taking care of shared gear, or doing a good turn for someone else.

## Scout Oath, Scout Law, and Motto

The Scout Oath and Scout Law guide how we treat one another and how we approach challenges.

The Cub Scout motto is **Do Your Best**. Scouts are encouraged to try, learn, improve, and take pride in honest effort.

Read the full [Scout Oath and Scout Law]({{ '/scout-oath-law/' | relative_url }}).

## Traditions

Cub Scouting includes traditions that help build identity and community, including flag ceremonies, campfires, songs, skits, advancement recognition, Pinewood Derby, Blue and Gold celebrations, and crossover ceremonies.

## Preparing for What Comes Next

Cub Scouts helps youth practice responsibility, resilience, teamwork, and outdoor skills. Older Cub Scouts prepare for Scouts BSA and future leadership opportunities while still enjoying a program built for their age and stage.
