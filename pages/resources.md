## Annual Registration

Returning families should expect an annual registration process that may include council registration, pack dues, medical or health forms, contact updates, and volunteer signups.

Watch pack communications for deadlines and links. If you have questions, contact us at [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## Uniforms and Gear

Families do not need to buy everything at once. Leaders can help explain what uniform pieces are needed, what can wait, and what gear is useful for meetings, hikes, and campouts.

Experienced uniforms or shared gear may be available. Ask before purchasing if you are unsure.

## Volunteer and Family Participation

The pack is strongest when many families each contribute a little. Some volunteers lead dens. Others organize events, help with communications, manage advancement records, coordinate camping trips, run activities, take photos, maintain equipment, or lend a hand when needed.

No one is expected to do everything, and no one volunteers alone. Training, resources, and support are available.
